Privacy Policy

×

Welcome to PrimeSYS. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our dashboard.


1. Information Collection
We do not collect unnecessary data. We only collect the basic Google account information (Name, Email, Profile Picture) strictly required for Authentication via Firebase. All personal notes, expenses, and tasks you create are securely synchronized directly to our cloud database.

2. Data Usage
Your data is used solely to provide you with the dashboard experience. We do NOT analyze your personal data, nor do we sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party advertisers.

3. Security
PrimeSYS uses Google's robust infrastructure (Firebase/Firestore) for end-to-end secure transmission of your data. We implement strict security protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Local-First Approach
Most of your data is processed locally on your device for lightning-fast speeds. The cloud is only used as a backup and synchronization layer.

5. Your Rights
You have full ownership of your data. You can delete your session or request complete data removal from our servers at any time through your Profile Settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

×
Is my data secure in PrimeSYS?
Yes, absolutely. We use Google Firebase for authentication and database synchronization. Your data is encrypted in transit and securely synced with Google's robust cloud infrastructure.
How do I backup my data offline?
You can easily export your data by navigating to Profile > Session and clicking the "Download Backup (JSON)" button. This downloads your entire dashboard data to your device. You can restore it anytime using the "Restore Backup" button.
Can I use PrimeSYS on multiple devices?
Yes! Simply log in with the same Google Account on any device, and your data (Notes, Tasks, Expenses) will automatically synchronize across all of them in real-time.
Does it work offline?
PrimeSYS is built with a local-first architecture. While an internet connection is required for initial login and syncing, most features like Notepad, Tasks, and Calculator run directly in your browser's memory for lightning-fast speeds.
How can I change my profile name?
Since we use Google Authentication, your display name and avatar are directly fetched from your Google Profile. If you update your Google Profile, it will automatically reflect in PrimeSYS on your next login.

Terminate Session?

This will permanently erase all your saved notes, calculations, and profile data from this browser. This action cannot be undone.
